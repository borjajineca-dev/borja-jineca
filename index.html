A LITTLE NOTE
TEACHERS' DAY
🌷

Dear Ma'am Christine,

Happy Teachers' Day! 🌷

I wanted to make this little website especially for you. Honestly, I was a little shy about making this because we don't really talk that often, and I didn't want to make it seem like I know your whole story when I don't. But I still wanted to create something instead of simply sending a greeting.

As a Computer Engineering student, I'm still learning how to make websites, so I thought, why not use the little skills I'm learning to make something for you? Minsan gumagana ang code, minsan hindi, and sometimes one tiny error makes you question your entire career as a programmer. 😭😂

But I really wanted to finish this because I wanted it to be something I made myself.

01
Beyond the classroom

I also wanted to recognize something beyond simply calling you a teacher.

From what I know, you carry many roles in your life. You're a teacher, a mother, a wife, and a person with your own responsibilities and dreams.

You have Kiara, Celestine, and Athena, and I'm sure being their mother is one of the most important parts of your life. You also have your husband, someone who shares this journey with you.

Being a teacher already comes with a lot of responsibility, but being a teacher while also being a mother and a wife must require a different kind of strength.

Especially when your children are far away, I can imagine that there are moments when you miss simply having them close. Yet you still continue to show up, teach, care, and do what you need to do every day.

02
A journey worth celebrating

Your journey as a Licensed Professional Teacher is something worth being proud of.

I also want to recognize your journey as a Licensed Professional Teacher. That is something you worked for and something worth being proud of.

Even though I wasn't there to witness that journey myself, I can appreciate what that achievement represents — your effort, dedication, and commitment to the profession you chose.

I know I'm not your former student, and I don't have years of classroom memories to share with you. But I don't think someone has to be your student to appreciate the person you are.

Sometimes, we can simply see the effort someone gives and respect them for it.

03
So... thank you.

This little website is my simple way of saying it.

Thank you for the work you do, for the students you teach, and for continuing to give your best even while carrying your own responsibilities outside the classroom.

And honestly, being a teacher, a mother, and a wife all at once? Ma'am, saan po kayo kumukuha ng energy? 😭😂 If it were me, baka low battery na ako by lunchtime.

I hope this little website reminds you that your effort is appreciated and that the things you do are worth celebrating.

Happy Teachers' Day, Ma'am Christine. 🤍🌷

Made with appreciation, Jineca Computer Engineering student 💻
